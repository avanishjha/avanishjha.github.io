Questions clients ask

How do you price work, and when do I pay?

Fixed scope, fixed price, quoted in writing after our first call. Projects start from ₹1.5 lakh, plus hosting and maintenance. There's no hourly meter, so you never pay for me to read documentation.

Payment follows milestones: usually 30% to start, the rest split across agreed milestones, and the last payment on handover. Hosting and care is a flat monthly fee.

What each service includes

Who owns the code and the infrastructure?

You do, all of it. The repo lives in your organisation from day one, and servers, domains and third-party accounts are registered in your name.

Full IP transfer on final payment is written into the agreement.

You're in Mumbai. Does the timezone work for me?

I work on IST (UTC+05:30), which overlaps well with Europe, the Middle East, the UK and APAC. For US clients I keep late-evening IST slots free for calls.

Written updates land in your inbox each morning, and there's always a staging URL to look at.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of support. If something I built breaks in that window, I fix it at no cost.

After that you can keep me on the monthly hosting and care plan, bring me back for specific work, or hand it to your own team with the runbook. Both of my client platforms are still live, and I still look after them.

More on hosting and care

Do you only work in Java?

No. Java and Spring Boot are my day job, but my client work has been in TypeScript: Next.js and React on the front, Node and NestJS behind, Postgres and Redis underneath, all in Docker. I pick what suits your project and your team.

And yes, I'm happy to work inside your existing codebase, with your repo, reviews and tracker.

Will you sign an NDA or a contract?

Yes, before you share anything sensitive. Send me yours, or I'll supply a mutual NDA and a contract covering scope, milestones, payment, IP transfer and confidentiality.

What if the project goes off the rails?

Milestones make it survivable. You're never more than one milestone away from a working, deployed build that you own, so the worst case is that you stop and keep everything built so far.

Scope changes are agreed in writing before they're built.
